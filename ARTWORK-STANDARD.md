## Prompt de arquitectura

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original art direction sheet for Auralis Frontier RTS, landscape 3D concept render, about 2400 by 1600. Original visual language, no copied game assets. Main subject: a cohesive small settlement blending naturally into rolling grass and a river bank, with distinct town hall, houses, well, granary, timber yard, mine entrance, smithy, stable, warehouse, watchtower, and wooden dock. Include physically legible footpaths, porches, load bays, workers carrying sacks and timber, pack horses, a small wagon, dockside wooden boat, contextual living details. Lower third: exactly ten evenly spaced small orthographic town-hall progression studies in two rows of five, from simple timber-and-plaster shelter to a developed stone-and-timber civic hall with increasing structural supports, porch, service wing, lookout and sober banner, same footprint and recognizable roof identity throughout. Medium detail rounded-edge 3D game geometry, matte materials, soft contact shadows, subdued daylight, warm wood, pale limestone, terracotta and muted slate, blue-teal faction cloth. Stylized but believable, approachable adult RTS art, never photorealistic, never plastic toy render. Buildings sit directly in the land with subtle dirt aprons, no display pedestals, no square floating grass platforms. At game scale clear silhouettes, foreground three-quarter orthographic camera shows fronts and right faces. No logos, no text, no numbers, no HUD, no watermarks, no dense microscopic decoration. Village scene and progression studies must share identical lighting, material family and camera angle.
```

Strong differences in architectural silhouette and equipment, not just palette swaps. Each diorama integrates directly into its terrain; no floating pedestal or giant square base. Maintain natural subdued colors and a blue-teal faction accent, no photorealism, no photographic landscapes, no oversized toy heads, no glossy plastic, no copied game characters, no logos, no labels, no HUD, no text, no watermarks.
